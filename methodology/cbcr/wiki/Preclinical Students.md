An important goal of the [[Case-Based Clinical Reasoning]] (CBCR) course for first-year medical students is to establish a foundational mental framework of illness scripts. The Utrecht CBCR test closely adheres to the cases discussed during these sessions, reinforcing and rehearsing learned concepts rather than expecting substantial transfer of learning skills to handle new or different cases early in their curriculum ch07_02_alignment-with-actual-cases-discussed.

For senior medical students who have already developed a basic mental framework of illness scripts through long-term memory, it may be beneficial to gradually test more general clinical reasoning abilities by deviating from previously discussed cases. However, this approach is not the primary objective for preclinical students at the end of their CBCR course.
