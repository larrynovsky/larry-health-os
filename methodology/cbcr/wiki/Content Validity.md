The Utrecht [[Case-Based Clinical Reasoning]] (CBCR) tests, as applied since 2010, have an undisputed content validity. This is because they are built upon cases that are used in the course and cover all relevant scenarios.

However, the construct validity of the Utrecht CBCR test approach remains under investigation. Construct validity refers to whether a test measures what it claims to measure ch03_02_early-thinking-of-clinical-reasoning-the-computer-analogy.
