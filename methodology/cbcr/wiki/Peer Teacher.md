In a [[Case-Based Clinical Reasoning]] (CBCR) education program, peer teachers play a crucial role in guiding sessions and discussions. These roles are assigned by turn at each session; every student fulfills the peer teacher task at least twice throughout the course ch06_08_the-consultant-role. Peer teachers lead meetings using the peer teacher version of the case which includes additional hints to help them prepare thoroughly before the session ch10_07_tasks-of-peer-teachers.

### Responsibilities and Tips
Peer teachers are expected to argue through all steps in the clinical decision-making process, engage every student in discussion, avoid giving premature information about how the case will proceed or answers students shouldn't have yet ch10_07_tasks-of-peer-teachers. They can use tools like a whiteboard for their mini-lectures and manage time effectively ch10_07_tasks-of-peer-teachers.

### Roles within [[Peer Teaching]]
Three distinct roles are identified: the chairperson, who leads discussions and ensures everyone participates; the summarizer, responsible for identifying key issues and providing conclusions at the end of questions; and the content expert, critical in evaluating student answers and seeking unresolved questions ch10_07_tasks-of-peer-teachers. These tasks should be alternated during the meeting to ensure a well-rounded evaluation by the consultant.

### Support from [[Consultant]]
While peer teachers lead, the consultant acts as an observer and only intervenes when necessary to clarify or provide perspective on rare conditions. The consultant assesses student preparation and active participation in clinical thinking during sessions ch06_08_the-consultant-role. They also distribute materials for upcoming sessions and maintain attendance records ch06_08_the-consultant-role.

### [[Introduction Session]]
An introductory session is held where the consultant explains the purpose of the CBCR course, sets rules, and assigns initial peer teachers. Peer teachers receive their first case in or after this introduction meeting ch10_04_introduction-session.

The value of CBCR lies in its ability to train students in hypothesis formulation based on new information received during sessions ch10_07_tasks-of-peer-teachers.
