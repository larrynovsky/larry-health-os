## Clinical Problem-Solving
### William Osler and [[Differential Diagnosis]]
William Osler, a pivotal figure in North American clinical medicine, introduced the concept of differential diagnosis during the early nineteenth century. This approach marked a shift from merely describing symptoms to drawing conclusions about underlying pathophysiological processes based on indirect information. The differential diagnosis is crucial for systematically considering various causes of a symptom, essential for effective clinical problem-solving ch02_04_william_osler_and_the_differential_diagnosis.

### Concepts and Definitions in [[Clinical Reasoning]]
Clinical reasoning involves both cognitive operations that enable observation, data collection, and analysis as well as decision-making tailored to individual patient circumstances.
