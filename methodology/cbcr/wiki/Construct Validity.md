The construct validity of the Utrecht [[Case-Based Clinical Reasoning]] (CBCR) Test approach remains an open question, despite its undisputed content validity since 2010. Content validity is ensured as the test cases are derived from educational materials used throughout the course and cover all relevant scenarios.

Construct validity refers to whether a test measures the theoretical construct it claims to measure—in this context, CBCR skills in clinical reasoning. The Utrecht approach does not yet have established evidence for construct validity, meaning further research is needed to confirm that it accurately assesses CBCR proficiency.
