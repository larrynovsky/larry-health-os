## Handout 3
Peer teachers distribute a handout with findings from all diagnostic tests (text in italics). Results are provided including their units and reference values, along with images for radiological results or ECGs if necessary. Peer teachers may need hints on how to interpret these results.
### Question 9: Interpret the Findings
Students interpret the test results and discuss the level of certainty they provide regarding hypotheses. Specificity and sensitivity of tests can be discussed here.
### Question 10: Which Diagnosis Prevails Now?
Students determine which diagnosis is most supported by current information, with consultants providing evidence-based answers where applicable.
### Question 11: Therapy and Prognosis
Given the diagnosis and patient circumstances, students propose appropriate therapy or care policies. The prognosis under both treated and untreated conditions should be considered.
#### Mini Lecture
One peer teacher provides a short presentation on relevant therapy options and their pros and cons.
### Role-Play
A role-play is optional but can help explain the cause of disease and rationale for proposed therapy to the patient in clear language. Students listen and comment afterwards.
## Stage V: Information About Therapy Policy Results
Handout 4 details the effects of the given therapy on the patient over time, written as a story. Depending on objectives, the case may continue with renewed patient presentations.
### Question 12: Summary of Case
A student summarizes the entire case chronologically in a few minutes to train summarization skills essential for clinical practice. Peer teachers can assist less advanced students using mnemonics or structured formats.
