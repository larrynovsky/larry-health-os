In practice, the [[CBCR Test|Utrecht CBCR test]] is administered twice a year, each instance contributing to half of the final score. Students must meet course requirements by achieving an adequate combined score from both the test and active participation in the course as both a student and a peer teacher. Participation itself is scored and contributes 12% towards the final grade, while the test score makes up 88%. The exact calculation for participation scores is detailed in the model study guide outlined in Chapter 10 of the Operation Log. Students who do not meet the requirements have an option to retake the examination.
