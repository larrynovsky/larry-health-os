In clinical reasoning, **deduction** is the process of testing a hypothesis through confirmation by expected diagnostic findings. If conditions X and Y are met, inference Z must be true ch03_03_deconstructing-the-reasoning-process. This method involves using prior knowledge to predict further findings that support or refute the initial diagnosis, such as confirming anemia with additional laboratory results like low serum iron levels or ferritin. Deduction is a critical part of the reasoning process alongside **abduction** and **induction**, forming a structured approach to diagnose patients accurately ch03_01_concepts-and-definitions.
