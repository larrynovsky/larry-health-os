# Introduction Session
In an introduction session, the students and their consultant get acquainted with each other. The consultant explains the purposes of [[ch01_03_essential features of cbcr education|CBCR]] course and gives the instructions for the sessions. Rules and regulations are set. The first three peer teachers are chosen, and they receive the peer teacher version of the first case.

This session sets the foundation for the upcoming [[ch06_04_rooms arrangement and facilities|sessions]], ensuring that all participants understand the objectives and expectations as outlined in [[ch10_02_the objectives of the cbcr course]]. The introduction also helps establish the context-specific approach to clinical reasoning, emphasizing [[ch03_09_case specificity and context specificity|case specificity]] and how it relates to [[ch08_04_peer teacher version|peer teaching roles]].

Understanding these initial steps is crucial for effective engagement in subsequent stages of the CBCR process. For instance, during [[ch06_10_evidence of effectiveness|evaluation phases]], participants can reflect on whether these foundational elements were adequately addressed and if they contributed to a better understanding of clinical reasoning.
