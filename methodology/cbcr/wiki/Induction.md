In the context of clinical reasoning, induction refers to the process of generalizing from multiple cases to create rules that can explain new cases ch03_03_deconstructing-the-reasoning-process. This method is more applicable in research and epidemiology than in individual patient care. Induction involves both forward-driven reasoning (hypothesis generation through data) and backward-driven reasoning (hypothesis testing) ch03_03_deconstructing-the-reasoning-process.
