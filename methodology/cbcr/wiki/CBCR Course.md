# [[Introduction Session]]
In an introduction session, the students and their consultant get acquainted with each other. The consultant explains the purposes of CBCR course and gives the instructions for the sessions. Rules and regulations are set. The first three peer teachers are chosen, and they receive the peer teacher version of the first case.
## The Objectives of the CBCR Course
CBCR focuses on learning to solve clinical problems by integrating knowledge from pathophysiology, epidemiology, and clinical decision-making. By successfully completing the course, students can reason clinically and systematically about patient problems presented in scenarios similar to those discussed in the CBCR cases. This includes evaluating collected data and making clear how they relate to a complaint/medical problem; using biomedical, epidemiological, and clinical knowledge in patient problems; making a focused differential diagnosis and evaluating all relevant hypotheses; giving a general direction for therapy or guidance.
In addition, students develop skills to handle new patient problems presented at the doctor’s office similar to CBCR cases. Leadership skills are also emphasized, as graduates can lead meetings on clinical reasoning.
## What Is [[Clinical Reasoning]] and Decision-Making?
Clinical reasoning involves rational considerations that underpin every step in a clinical encounter from when a patient presents at the doctor's office until the end of contact. The process often involves solving medical questions/problems with arguments that include both pathophysiological and non-pathophysiological factors.
## The [[Consultant]] Role
The consultant oversees CBCR sessions, records attendance, assesses students' active participation, provides feedback, distributes materials for future sessions, and completes evaluation forms. Their role is adaptive, guiding the session when peer teachers need support but remaining in the background most of the time to encourage student responsibility and clinical thinking.
### Participant Roles
All participants should be familiar with rules before starting the CBCR course. An introductory session helps set expectations and provide instructions.
