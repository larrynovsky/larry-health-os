The **student version** of a CBCR ([[Case-Based Clinical Reasoning]]) case includes general instructions, an initial case vignette, and several questions. The instructions specify the objectives for this case, the literature students should read to prepare, and which questions they must answer before attending the session ch08_02_three-versions-of-the-written-case. This version is provided to all participants, including peer teachers who also need to engage in clinical reasoning themselves during the CBCR sessions.
