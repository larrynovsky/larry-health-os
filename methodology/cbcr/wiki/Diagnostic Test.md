# Stage IV: The Results of Diagnostic Tests Are Provided
Handout 3 is distributed which includes findings from all diagnostic tests, including their units and reference values. Radiological or ECG results may be presented as images if necessary. Peer teachers provide hints to guide students in interpreting test results and discussing their implications for the differential diagnosis.
Students are asked to interpret these findings and assess how much certainty they offer regarding hypotheses. They also evaluate which diagnosis prevails now based on the new information, considering therapy options tailored to patient circumstances. Consultants are provided with evidence-based answers where applicable.
A mini lecture may be given by a peer teacher on relevant therapy options, followed by a role-play involving one student as the doctor and another as the patient discussing the proposed treatment plan in simple terms. This helps students practice summarizing medical problems clearly.
Handout 4 is then read out loud to provide information about the effects of the prescribed therapy over time. Students summarize the entire case chronologically, practicing efficient summary skills essential for clinical years.
If the diagnostic test results differ, an alternative scenario can be presented starting from any stage (II, III, or IV), maintaining compatibility with initial conditions. Writing high-quality CBCR cases requires significant effort but yields long-term educational benefits and scholarly output.
