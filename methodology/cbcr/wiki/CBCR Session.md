# A CBCR Session
A CBCR session lasts between **1 hour 45 minutes** and **2 hours 30 minutes**. Typically, one to two patient cases are discussed during a single session.
### Facilitation by [[Peer Teachers]]
Three peer teachers usually lead the discussion, although it is possible but not recommended for fewer or more than three due to practicality concerns. Students must have read preparatory literature before the meeting, such as clinical practice guidelines and general practice handbooks. This ensures they can contribute effectively to case discussions.
### Role of Peer Teachers
Before a session begins, each peer teacher reads out the initial patient vignette, introduces the patient's complaint, and guides students through answering questions based on their prepared materials. They also provide additional information via handouts and deliver one mini-lecture per session (5–10 minutes) to summarize key points of the case.
### Student Participation
Students are randomly selected to give answers and substantiate them during discussions. At the end of each session, a non-peer teaching student summarizes the case in 2-3 minutes for handover purposes.
## Participant Roles
Before starting CBCR sessions, all participants should be familiar with the rules and objectives through an introductory meeting. This helps set expectations and ensures smooth execution.
### Tasks of Peer Teachers
Peer teachers are assigned roles at the introduction session and rotate throughout the course. They prepare cases thoroughly using detailed versions provided by a consultant, allowing them to guide discussions, evaluate student responses, and provide well-founded answers during sessions. Each peer teacher delivers a mini-lecture on specific diagnoses, tests, or therapies.
#### Meeting Structure
1. **Introduction**: Patient introduction and problem formulation.
2. **Answering**: Students answer initial questions individually or in small groups.
3. **Inventory of Answers**: Key arguments are documented.
4. **Reflection on Responses**: Peer teachers reflect and provide feedback using mini-lectures if necessary.
5. **Brief Summary**: A student summarizes the case at session end.
6. **Evaluation**: The consultant evaluates participation, distributes materials for future sessions, discusses outcomes, and assesses overall performance of peer teachers.
### Tips for Peer Teachers
Peer teachers should encourage active participation from all students, avoid giving away future case details to unprepared groups, use external resources wisely, manage time effectively, alternate roles (chairperson, summarizer, content expert) within the session, and submit feedback to consultants. This enhances learning outcomes and develops leadership skills among participants.
